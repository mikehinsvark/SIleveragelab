01

Think + create

ChatGPT

ChatGPT is OpenAI’s conversational AI assistant for turning questions, ideas, and raw information into useful work. It helps you draft and refine writing, reason through decisions, explain complex topics, summarize documents, and analyze data. Depending on available tools, it can also search the web and work with images—making it a versatile thinking partner for everyday business tasks.

Strengths
Flexible conversation, clear explanations, creative drafting, document analysis, and data exploration in one adaptable workspace.
Key uses
Draft client emails, practice sales conversations, summarize documents, brainstorm campaigns, and explore spreadsheet trends.

02

Reason + refine

Claude.ai

Claude is Anthropic’s general-purpose AI assistant for thoughtful writing, reasoning, coding, and document work. Give it context, files, or a complex question, then collaborate on drafts, summaries, analysis, and deliverables you can review and refine. Its broad workflow support makes it useful for synthesizing detailed material and developing polished business communication rather than simply producing a quick answer.

Strengths
Context-rich writing, structured reasoning, file analysis, coding assistance, and thoughtful revision of detailed business materials.
Key uses
Synthesize customer feedback, turn meeting notes into action plans, review documents, and draft reports, proposals, or presentations.
Open Claude ↗
03

Operate + automate

Grok Bot

Grok Bot is xAI’s task-oriented AI agent for taking work beyond a chat response. It uses a persistent cloud computer to interact with apps, websites, and connected tools, carry out multi-step tasks, and learn repeatable routines. With the right permissions, it can coordinate specialist bots and work across interfaces—even when a tool does not offer a straightforward API.

Strengths
Computer-level task execution, persistent context, repeatable routines, and coordination across apps, websites, and specialist bots.
Key uses
Research sales accounts, prepare personalized outreach, organize invoice workflows, and reproduce software issues with supporting evidence.
Open Grok ↗
04

Learn + synthesize

Gemini Notebook

Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM, is Google’s source-grounded AI research notebook. Add documents, web pages, audio, and other materials, then ask questions, extract insights, and turn dense information into useful briefs or learning formats. Its distinctive advantage is working from the sources you choose and providing attribution, so you can trace important answers back to the supporting material.

Strengths
Source-grounded answers, traceable attribution, synthesis across multiple materials, and accessible formats for learning and research.
Key uses
Compare market research, query policy documents, create product-training briefs, and turn business source material into study aids.
Open Notebook ↗
05

Watch + learn

YouTube

YouTube is a video-sharing and learning platform—not an LLM. It brings searchable demonstrations, tutorials, interviews, livestreams, podcasts, and short-form content into one destination. For business builders, it works both as an on-demand classroom and a publishing channel: learn a skill visually, explain your product, and build an audience through a library of useful, shareable video content.

Strengths
Searchable visual learning and channel-based publishing across short videos, long-form content, livestreams, and podcasts.
Key uses
Learn software workflows, publish product demonstrations, create customer tutorials, share expert interviews, and educate prospects.
Open YouTube ↗
06

Plan + execute

Gemini

Gemini is Google’s multimodal AI assistant for writing, brainstorming, learning, planning, and research. You can work conversationally with text, voice, or images, summarize information, and connect supported Google services where available. Its strength is combining flexible AI assistance with the Google ecosystem, helping you move from scattered inputs to a clearer draft, plan, or next action.

Strengths
Text, voice, and image input; flexible drafting and summarization; and connections to supported Google services.
Key uses
Draft customer messages, summarize project materials, brainstorm campaigns, compare ideas, and prepare meeting agendas or working plans.
Open Gemini ↗
07

Delegate + coordinate

Meta Muse

Muse is Meta’s personal AI agent for multi-step everyday and small-business tasks. Through chat and supported connected apps, it can help organize information and delegate work using a dedicated cloud computer with browser access. It is designed to move beyond advice into action while keeping you involved through permissions and approvals—not simply generate text or host video.

Strengths
App-connected task coordination, a dedicated browser environment, and user permission controls for multi-step delegated work.
Key uses
Triage email, coordinate appointments, research options, prepare travel plans, and organize small-business follow-through with review.
Open Meta Muse ↗
08

System + leverage

AI Leverage Lab

AI Leverage Lab is Dreams Business Resources’ live, 12-week AI training program for independent producers and business builders—not a standalone LLM. It teaches practical, no-code ways to use multiple AI tools through weekly classes, industry-specific demonstrations, and applied assignments. The emphasis is on building usable workflows for prospecting, follow-up, content, learning, and administrative work rather than chasing every new tool.

Strengths
Hands-on, no-code instruction across several AI tools, grounded in real prospecting, follow-up, and client-service workflows.
Key uses
Develop prospect research routines, create lead follow-up sequences, produce client content, and improve meeting preparation.
Open AI Leverage Lab ↗
09

Connect + grow

Dreams Cloud Bundle

Dreams Cloud Bundle is a business-marketing and agency-services package presented within Dreams Cloud 3—not a standalone AI model. Its listed components bring together website presence, AI optimization, prospect identification, multichannel follow-up, sales-assistant support, CRM tools, and coaching. The value is a connected business stack for coordinating marketing and customer outreach; the linked package page explains the available components and options.

Strengths
A bundled approach to website presence, prospecting, multichannel outreach, CRM organization, coaching, and sales-support tools.
Key uses
Identify prospects, coordinate email/text/call follow-up, organize leads, and manage local marketing campaigns through a website and CRM.
